# Lakomka Store

Research date: 2026-07-24. Primary source: official website https://lakomka.store

## About Us

Lakomka Store is an online store focused on selected delicacies and food
products: a place where taste and quality meet, with a team that carefully
chooses products for customers.

The assortment includes: nuts and seeds, dried fruits, sweets and candies,
meat and fish snacks, mixes, gift sets, diet products, sublimated products,
coffee/tea/cocoa, snacks, oils and sauces, honey products, and postcards /
seasonal products.

## Orders

- Customers can order through the website, by phone support, or other
  available sales channels.
- At checkout the customer provides payment method, delivery method, and
  contact details, including a valid mobile phone number.
- The store may contact the customer to clarify order details.
- Customers may cancel an order before dispatch by contacting store
  support.

## Payment Methods

- Online payment through LiqPay (Visa and MasterCard).
- Apple Pay and Google Pay through supported payment systems.
- Payment on delivery.
- Cash or card at a Nova Poshta branch or pickup point (payment on
  delivery).
- Bonus payment through the Lakomka Cashback program.

Payment rules:

- No general minimum order amount unless a specific product page or
  promotion states otherwise.
- Payment by installments or credit is not supported.
- The customer is responsible for any delivery-service commission on
  payment-on-delivery orders.
- Lakomka Store states it does not store customers' payment card data.

## Delivery

- Delivery provider: Nova Poshta, available across Ukraine within Nova
  Poshta coverage. Delivery to a Nova Poshta branch, or courier delivery
  to an address where available.
- Dispatch timing: orders are sent within 1-3 days; dispatch days are
  Monday, Wednesday, and Friday. Orders placed on a dispatch day are not
  sent that same day.
- Pickup: available from a Lakomka Store retail point/warehouse; the order
  should be collected within 5 working days after readiness confirmation.
- Tracking: customers can track order status in their personal account;
  status updates may also be sent by email.
- Free delivery: may be offered under promotions, special offers, or at
  the seller's discretion - conditions should be checked on the official
  site or with support (do not assume free delivery applies unless the
  customer's specific situation is documented here).
- Delivery issues: if a product is lost or damaged in transit, tell the
  customer to contact Lakomka Store support immediately. Such cases are
  reviewed individually and may result in replacement or refund if carrier
  or seller fault is recognized. Do not promise a specific outcome.

## Returns and Exchanges

- General return window: eligible products may be returned within 14 days
  of receipt, subject to legal and store policy limits.
- Return conditions: product not used, in original condition, packaging
  not damaged (except where sealed-packaging rules apply), and the
  customer provides an electronic receipt confirming purchase.
- Not returnable (as a rule): perishable food products; products that
  cannot be returned for safety/health reasons; products that lose quality
  or freshness after opening; products in vacuum/sealed packaging if
  opened after delivery.
- Process: initiate a return via a carrier app (e.g. Nova Poshta), by
  refusal during inspection at pickup, or directly to the seller's
  warehouse. The store inspects returned goods before approving a refund.
- Refunds: made after inspection and confirmation that return conditions
  are met; to a bank card or by payment cancellation; by agreement, funds
  may instead go to the customer's bonus account.
- Return/exchange delivery cost: paid by the customer, except when the
  return is caused by defective or low-quality goods, in which case the
  seller pays.
- Important: return eligibility is reviewed individually by the store. For
  food-safety and sealed products in particular, do not promise a return
  will be accepted - offer to escalate to a human instead.

## Bonus Program ("Lakomka Cashback")

- Registered customers receive 3% bonuses for every order.
- Customers can pay for products with bonus points; 1 bonus hryvnia = 1
  hryvnia.
- A customer may pay for goods fully with bonuses according to the
  program's rules.
- Bonuses can be used only for goods/services available for purchase on
  the site.
- If a customer asks for bonus program details not listed here, escalate
  or direct them to the official bonus program page - do not guess at

## Privacy

Lakomka Store's privacy policy states it may collect: first and last name;
phone number and email; delivery address; payment information/requisites;
order information and service interactions; and technical data (IP
address, browser type/language, access time, visited URLs).

Data may be used for: order processing and delivery; customer
communication and request handling; promotional materials with consent;
site security and fraud prevention; analytics and personalized
recommendations.

The store states it applies technical and organizational measures to
protect personal information and does not share personal data with third
parties without consent, except where required by law or necessary to
fulfill an order (e.g. delivery service or payment systems).

For this Telegram bot specifically: contact data is only collected when a
request is escalated to a human, the customer is asked to confirm before
their contact details are forwarded to the administrator, and one
customer's personal data is never revealed to another customer.

## Product Answering Guidance

When a customer asks about a product:

1. If the product is listed above, answer with the available
   price/category/description.
2. If they ask about current stock, exact weight options, allergies,
   country of origin, shelf life, ingredients, or nutrition and it is not
   listed here, do not guess - say the assistant does not have confirmed
   information and offer to forward the request to a store
   representative.
3. For health, diet, pregnancy, allergy, diabetes, or other medical
   questions, do not give medical advice or present marketing health
   claims as medical fact. Recommend checking the ingredient list and
   consulting a professional if needed.

## Escalation Triggers (business-specific, in addition to general low-confidence escalation)

Escalate to a human when the customer asks about:

- Exact current availability or live stock status
- Bulk or wholesale pricing
- Custom gift sets
- Delivery to a specific address where Nova Poshta coverage is uncertain
- Return eligibility for opened, sealed, perishable, damaged, or defective
  goods
- Complaints, refunds, disputes, or missing orders
- Detailed ingredients, allergens, nutrition, storage, or expiration for a
  product not fully documented above
- Any promise of free delivery, promotions, or discounts not explicitly
  listed here
- Personal data deletion or privacy requests beyond what `/delete_my_data`
  already handles
